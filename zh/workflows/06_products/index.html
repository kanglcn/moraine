    06 Deformation products¶

    Pipeline: examples/06_products.toml. Tutorial: nbs/Tutorials/CLI/CampiFlegrei/06_products.ipynb. Needs 04_refine and 05_unwrap. The steps and conventions are decision 0040.

    moraine run examples/06_products.toml --workdir WORK

    Set [vars] ref_area to a reference area of the data (a numexpr expression of lon and lat, the longitude and latitude of the points) and wavelength to the radar wavelength in meters (radar_wavelength of raw/meta.toml); the defaults are the ones of the sample data set (a box east of the caldera, Sentinel-1 0.0554658 m).

    Steps¶

    1. math: the reference points, a bool mask of the points inside ref_area. A polygon drawn on a map (mc.view(..., polygons='reference.geojson'), or written with moraine.write_polygons) works too:
    [[step]]
name = "ref_mask"
run = "polygon-mask"
polygons = "reference.geojson"
x = "pc/pc_lon.zarr"
y = "pc/pc_lat.zarr"
mask = "disp/pc_ref_mask.zarr"
    1. pc-reference: from every image the median of the reference points is subtracted, so that it is 0 in every image; method = "mean" takes their mean, ref_point (instead of ref_mask) one point, e.g. the point of a double click on a map. The median is not moved by a few reference points with unwrapping errors.
    2. pc-disp: line of sight displacement in millimeters, 1000 * wavelength / (4 pi) per radian (4.41 mm for Sentinel-1), positive towards the satellite (a shorter range).
    3. pc-pyramid of the displacement on the map.

    Outputs in WORK/disp/:

    • pc_ref_mask.zarr (n_points,) bool: the reference points.
    • pc_ts_ref.zarr (n_points, nimages) float32: the unwrapped phase of every image relative to the first image and to the reference area, in radians.
    • ref_value.zarr (nimages,) float32: the value subtracted from every image, in radians.
    • pc_disp.zarr (n_points, nimages) float32: the line of sight displacement in millimeters relative to the first image and to the reference area, positive towards the satellite. It is along the line of sight, not vertical: a vertical motion u gives about u cos(incidence) (incidence about 35 degrees on the sample data), plus a share of the horizontal motion.

    The reference area¶

    The displacements are relative to the reference area, so its own motion is in every point. Take an area that is stable (outside the deforming area), coherent (many refined points), not affected by strong local atmosphere, close to the area of interest and at a similar height (MintPy's guidelines), and inside one region of the closure correction (unw/pc_region.zarr): points of other regions can be off by whole cycles in some images, which the loops do not detect. Check the regions of the reference points in python:

    import numpy as np, zarr
region = zarr.open('unw/pc_region.zarr', mode='r')[:]
ref = zarr.open('disp/pc_ref_mask.zarr', mode='r')[:]
np.unique(region[ref], return_counts=True)

    The sample scene is small: every part of it is within about 10 km of Pozzuoli, so no area is far from the uplift. The default box east of the caldera (Naples, 8-13 km from Pozzuoli) has 11 131 points, 99.7 % of them in the region of Pozzuoli, and is flat inside (its points 9-10, 10-11 and 11-13 km from Pozzuoli differ by less than 1 mm in the last image). Areas in the north-west and the north of the scene move by 12-15 mm less than it over the three years, and only 49 % (north-west) and 76 % (north) of their points are in that region; with them as reference all displacements shift by that much. Which area is stable is not known from the data alone (GNSS stations would tell).

    log of reference reference values min -3.5, median 0.31, max 5.2 (radians) the level of every image, a few radians

    Run time: pc-reference 1.0 s, pc-disp 0.8 s, the pyramid 4.2 s; 9.6 s and 0.66 GB peak memory for the pipeline. Large data (10 million points, 92 images, 4 threads): pc-reference 10.3 s and 0.9 GB, pc-disp 15.5 s and 0.97 GB.

    Checks¶

    • moraine info disp/pc_disp_pyramid: only the warning for image 0; values within a few hundred millimeters.
    • moraine quicklook disp/pc_disp_pyramid --index I -o disp.png: about 0 in the reference area, smooth; the sign of a known motion right (uplift: positive).
    • moraine view disp/pc_disp_pyramid --dates raw/meta.toml -o view.ipynb: click points for their displacement time series.

    Problems¶

    • pc-reference fails with "no value (all nan)": the reference points have no data in an image; take another area.
    • pc-disp fails with "between 0 and 1": the wavelength is not in meters (e.g. 5.55 in centimeters).
    • Jumps of about 27.7 mm (half a wavelength for Sentinel-1) between areas or dates are whole cycles of unwrapping errors: check the regions and the change fraction of 05_unwrap.
    • The DEM error, the velocity and the residual of every image are not computed yet (decision 0040, later steps).
